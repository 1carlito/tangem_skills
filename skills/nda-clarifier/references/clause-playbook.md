# NDA Clause Playbook

This file defines the review inventory. It does not contain approved legal positions.
Populate `assets/clause-library-template.yaml` with organization-approved rules.

## Administrative terms

Check:

- Correct legal names and entity types
- Effective date and signature date
- Mutual, receiving-party, or disclosing-party structure
- Stated purpose and permitted use
- Entire agreement, amendments, notices, counterparts, and electronic signatures

Incorrect party identity, missing pages, or unclear NDA direction prevents standard routing.

## Confidential information

Review separately:

- Definition and scope
- Oral disclosures and marking requirements
- Information disclosed before the effective date
- Information created through observation or access
- Personal data, source code, security information, and other special categories
- Affiliates and representatives included as disclosers or recipients

Do not assume a broad or narrow definition is acceptable without an applicable rule.

## Exclusions

Identify each exclusion and its burden-of-proof language:

- Publicly available information
- Prior lawful possession
- Independent development
- Lawful third-party receipt
- Discloser-authorized release

Flag missing exclusions or altered evidentiary requirements according to the library.

## Use and protection obligations

Review:

- Purpose limitation
- Standard of care
- Access limited to need-to-know representatives
- Recipient responsibility for representatives
- Security requirements
- Incident or unauthorized-disclosure notice
- Copying and reproduction restrictions
- Reverse engineering, decompilation, or analysis restrictions

Do not infer operational feasibility. Ask the business or security owner when the library
requires factual confirmation.

## Permitted and compelled disclosure

Review:

- Disclosure to employees, affiliates, advisers, contractors, financing sources, or buyers
- Legal compulsion process
- Notice to the discloser
- Protective-order cooperation
- Minimum-necessary disclosure
- Cost allocation

Record when notice is limited by law or court order.

## Duration and survival

Extract separately:

- Agreement term
- Disclosure period
- Confidentiality survival period
- Treatment of trade secrets
- Renewal and termination mechanics

Never collapse multiple time periods into a single “term” finding.

## Return and destruction

Review:

- Trigger and deadline
- Certification requirement
- Backup, archive, legal-hold, and compliance exceptions
- Continuing protection of retained copies
- Technical feasibility dependencies

## Ownership and intellectual property

Review:

- No license or transfer of ownership
- Feedback rights
- Residual knowledge
- Developments, improvements, or invention assignment
- Reverse-engineering restrictions

Route any position according to the approved library; do not draft an IP compromise.

## Commercial and liability terms

Identify:

- No obligation to proceed
- No warranty or reliance
- Limitation of liability
- Indemnity
- Injunctive or equitable relief
- Fees, costs, or prevailing-party provisions

These may be outside a routine NDA playbook. Unknown or unsupported terms route to legal.

## Restrictive and transaction terms

Identify:

- Non-solicitation
- Non-competition
- No-hire
- Standstill
- Exclusivity
- Non-circumvention
- Publicity or use of name
- Contact restrictions

Do not treat these as confidentiality boilerplate. Apply an explicit library rule or route.

## Regulatory and data terms

Identify:

- Privacy and data-protection obligations
- Data-processing terms
- Export controls and sanctions
- Security standards or audit rights
- Regulated-data requirements
- Required policy incorporation by reference

If another agreement or policy is incorporated but not supplied, mark the document incomplete.

## Assignment, law, and disputes

Review:

- Assignment and change-of-control treatment
- Governing law
- Venue, jurisdiction, arbitration, and waiver provisions
- Conflict-of-laws wording

Applicability is entity- and jurisdiction-specific. Never apply a rule outside its scope.

## Completeness checks

Before classification:

- Reconcile defined terms with actual usage.
- Locate every cross-reference and incorporated document.
- Confirm exhibits and signature pages are present.
- Confirm no handwritten or image-only changes were missed.
- Compare redlines against the correct base version when supplied.
- Record duplicate or conflicting clauses rather than choosing one silently.
